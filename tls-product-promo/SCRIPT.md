# Script

TLS is invisible, until you can watch it move.

This interactive demo turns every handshake into a live map: client, server, packet, key.

Follow ClientHello, certificate proof, E C D H E secrets, and Finished checks.

Then see plain H T T P become authenticated encrypted data.

Teach TLS without hand waving.

## Notes

- Target duration: 20 seconds.
- Spoken text is saved in `narration.txt`.
- Tone: clear, confident, product-promo energy without overselling.
